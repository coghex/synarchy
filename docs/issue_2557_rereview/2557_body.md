## Background

Epic #2526, slice EFM-10. Depends on #2555 (condition seam and probe),
#2539 (corpse policy), and #2549/#2552 (wild/row and plot persistence).
Treat those prerequisites and their canonical review amendments as
delivered contracts. This issue implements natural-death retention,
expiry, durable successors, and identity-specific row removal in one PR,
including its documentation, migration fixtures, and validation evidence.

Verified against `master` `65ad8231916a` on 2026-09-10:

- `World.Flora.Growth` derives growth age from placement age plus calendar
  days times `growthRate(health)`. Its `deadWindowDays = 60` is currently
  60 units of health-scaled growth age, not 60 elapsed calendar days.
  Annual/biennial/perennial instances wrap after lifespan plus that window;
  evergreens do not die naturally.
- `CropPlot.cropPlotInstance` uses the same growth function with elapsed
  days since planting. Without a new transition, natural crop death also
  wraps into another generation automatically.
- `World.Thread.Time` advances only visible pages. `world.setDate` changes
  the addressed page's canonical date and invalidates quads but performs
  no retention reconciliation.
- Row crops replay from identity-bearing placement edits. No removal edit
  currently prevents their return after regeneration. The `world-edits`
  component is v3 with retained v1/v2 codecs.
- Permanent flora removal clears identity-keyed harvest/designation state;
  chunk eviction deliberately retains it. Groundcover plots are page-owned
  tile-keyed values, and existing Lua harvest/plant operations can mutate
  them directly.

Owner decision 2026-09-10: **transient retention uses elapsed calendar
days, independent of plant health**. This intentionally changes the legacy
reseeding dates of plants below full health. An annual with initial age 0
and health 0.5 dies on day 576: a 60-day policy now expires on day 636,
whereas the old health-scaled wrap reseeded on day 672. Do not retain the
previous claim that all shipped nonpersistent behavior is unchanged.

## Requirements

1. **Retention clock.** Select the species' `corpsePolicy`, including
   phase/cause overrides, and interpret `durationDays` as elapsed days on
   the owning page's calendar. Health continues to scale living growth;
   it does not scale corpse retention. The defaulted legacy policy means
   transient 60 calendar days with wild successor `reseed`, under the
   same rule as an authored policy. Preserve accepted finite fractional
   durations and their exact stored expiry; apply the transition at the
   first evaluated page day at or beyond that expiry.

   An already stored retention outcome remains authoritative: do not
   recompute or retime it from current content. Persistent outcomes have
   no expiry. Preserve existing lifespan selection and evergreen natural
   immortality; persistent corpse policy does not itself kill a plant.

2. **Natural death is durable.** Natural death selects cause `natural`,
   the context-appropriate successor, and the last living phase and
   optional annual stage at the death boundary, using the prerequisite
   selector contract. `PhaseDead` is not a phase-at-death value. Preserve
   that selected retention, successor, and frozen phase/stage through
   save/load, eviction, regeneration, and later edits to authored corpse
   policy. Neither observing the corpse later nor reloading it restarts
   its timer or resnapshots its policy.

   This applies to wild flora, planted rows, and groundcover plots without
   requiring an external condition command. Both natural and explicitly
   conditioned deaths use the same authoritative retention/successor
   semantics. Persistent remains do not wrap into a new generation merely
   because the old dead window elapsed.

3. **Calendar reconciliation.** Reconcile natural mortality and stored
   outcomes through the world-owned transition boundary on day rollover
   and `world.setDate`, including date assignments while paused. Every
   page uses its own date and calendar. Evaluating another loaded page
   must not advance its clock or borrow the visible page's date.

   A jump across death or expiry must produce the same outcome as
   evaluating the intervening boundaries. A reseed is anchored to its
   expiry day, with age 0 there and subsequent growth derived from that
   anchor; observing it after a jump must not restart growth at age 0 on
   the observation day. Preserve subsequent natural-death boundaries as
   well. Repeated or backward date assignments must not undo completed
   durable death/successor transitions, recreate an expired corpse, or
   execute the same successor again. Invalidate affected render caches
   when effective state changes.

4. **Context-specific successors.** Before expiry, the occurrence remains
   dead with its frozen phase/stage. At expiry, apply its selected
   successor exactly once:

   - Wild `reseed`: retain the generation/age anchor needed for the new
     generation to grow correctly across regeneration and save/load.
   - Wild `absent`: retain a durable tombstone that excludes the occurrence
     from drawing, hit testing, harvest, queries for existing flora, and AI.
   - Cultivated row: remove that planted occurrence durably through an
     identity-specific removal edit, allowing deliberate replanting.
   - Cultivated groundcover plot: delete the expired plot and its embedded
     condition, leaving its tilled tile available for deliberate replanting.

   Both cultivated outcomes are `await_replanting`; they ignore the
   species' wild `successor` and never replant a field automatically.
   Persistent remains are not expired. Clearing an explicit condition
   through #2555 must not reset growth/planting time, revive a naturally
   dead occurrence, or erase a completed durable successor. Preserve
   authored wood-harvest eligibility and the existing chop/fell behavior;
   this issue adds no player cleanup action.

5. **Removal and replacement safety.** Removal addresses the owning page,
   canonical tile, and exact `FloraInstanceId`, preserving same-tile
   co-tenants and unrelated pages. Record it durably even when the row's
   chunk is evicted, so replay cannot recreate that occurrence. Repeated
   removal is harmless: `place A / remove A / place B / remove A` leaves
   B intact. Preserve oldest-first edit replay and a monotonically
   advancing planted-ID allocator; removal never makes an ID reusable.

   Clear obsolete harvest timers and designations for the removed
   identity, preserving unrelated entries. Eviction alone is not removal.
   Plot expiry must preserve unrelated entries and must neither delete a
   replacement nor restore a removed plot from a stale snapshot. Replanting
   creates fresh living row/plot state with no inherited corpse condition.

6. **Bounded semantic state.** Keep bounded current retention/successor
   state per occurrence rather than accumulating a history of generations.
   Compact a marker or tombstone only when its removal preserves current
   and future observable behavior, including presence, age, phase/stage,
   generation progression, and future death/expiry boundaries. Equal
   generation numbers alone are not sufficient. Retain durable absence or
   a reseed anchor indefinitely when it is still necessary; eventual
   deletion of every record is not an acceptance requirement.

7. **Persistence and compatibility.** Append the removal constructor to
   `WorldEdit` and its current DTO. Move `world-edits` from v3 to v4,
   freezing the complete outgoing v3 wire shape and preserving every
   retained decoder and migration. If intervening prerequisite work has
   advanced this component, migrate from its actual current version to
   the next without replacing that work. Preserve durable species-name
   references, canonical coordinates, and allocator validation, including
   IDs named by removal edits.

   Append `reseeded`/`absent` condition values using the prerequisite's
   semantic record without changing its serialized field layout or
   requiring another `world-activity` version. Preserve all historical
   nested wire shapes. Apply the repository's `currentSaveVersion` rule
   if the transitional serialized runtime bridge changes; that does not
   replace the component migration.

   Generate and register a current complete-session fixture containing
   the new removal and successor state through
   `tools/save_compat_audit.py --generate-session`; include its exact
   invocation, production-path setup, and provenance in the PR. Retain
   existing fixtures and prove older edits still migrate. Preserve
   prerequisite validation and missing-definition failures, including
   semantic records whose chunks are unloaded.

8. **Documentation and integration evidence.** Update
   `docs/persistence_state_inventory.md`, `docs/persistence_contract.md`
   §4, the retention section of `docs/flora_visual_state_contract.md`,
   and `docs/engine_contracts.md` §Flora visual state and fallback.
   Document calendar-day retention, its intentional compatibility change,
   natural and cultivated successors, date reconciliation, and compaction
   rules. Required documentation, fixtures, and evidence belong in this
   implementation PR before final review and merge.

the production growth, condition, date-command, edit-replay, and
capture/restore boundaries. Cover:

- Natural and explicit deaths for transient reseed, transient absent, and
  persistent synthetic policies; wild, row, and plot contexts; day before,
  at, and after expiry; and natural cultivated death without an explicit
  condition record. Evergreen natural behavior remains unchanged.

  reseed at day 636 for the half-health annual above. Include a valid

  multi-day jumps crossing death and expiry; equivalent successor/growth
