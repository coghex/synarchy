## Background

FTS-6A of #2496, Phase 12, critical path. **Depends on #2558 (FTS-6E),
which must merge first.** #2561 (FTS-6F) is adjacent and may land before
or after this issue. Deliver the incident/report integration, persistence,
tests, and required documentation together in one PR.

Verified on `master` `36090b94a39a` (2026-09-10):

- `unit.getLastAttacker` exposes the latest runtime attacker UID and
  timestamp without consuming or expiring them. Lua uses this memory for
  short-term retaliation; the Haskell fields reset on load.
- `scripts/unit_ai_notify.lua` supplies delayed radio broadcasting and
  rank-split walking notification for water knowledge. Its water
  recipients are currently acolytes.
- `Unit.Faction.Profile.relationFromTo` evaluates matching tags and live
  causes, without page or incident-knowledge inputs. A cause on a shared
  group tag therefore affects every member carrying that tag.
- `unit_ai_save.lua` persists per-unit Lua AI state; the shared
  `unit_ai_ref_schema.lua` drives reference validation and reconciliation.

The prerequisite chain supplies the live cause store/API, minted roster
and occupant group tags, and Attack/Hold escalation behavior. Their
implementation belongs to those prerequisites.

**Owner decision 2026-09-10:** the first successful report delivered to a
living teammate makes the **entire minted group hostile**, including
unreached members on other pages. Incident details remain limited to
units that directly observed or received the incident. Group relation
and per-unit incident knowledge are separate facts.

## Requirements

1. **Observe and identify incidents.** Store incident knowledge in each
   informed unit's Lua AI state. A living damaged unit learns from
   `unit.getLastAttacker`; a living same-group witness learns when its
   AI tick observes the victim damaged or dead with the victim's tile
   visible on the witness's own page. Equal coordinates on different
   pages never establish witnessing. A dead unit records and reports
   nothing; an unwitnessed kill leaves no incident on survivors.

   Every observed incident has one stable identity shared by the victim
   and witnesses, retained through reporting and load. Distinguish
   different victims and different observed attacker stamps; repeated
   polling of an unchanged stamp must not create new incidents. Capture
   the victim/attacker identity, incident time, explicit group scopes,
   and reported state consistently across copies. Repeated observations
   of the same stamp, including observing its victim dead, do not create
   a second report cause. This remains an observation-based feature;
   reconstructing attacks overwritten between AI observations is outside
   its scope.

2. **Use explicit minted group scopes.** Controlled units use the roster
   team tag supplied by #2554; uncontrolled location members use its
   occupant group tag. Capture the attacker's corresponding group tag
   when the incident is recorded, or its absence. Reports never choose a
   culture tag or guess a primary tag from arbitrary memberships. Preserve
   the captured incident identity and tags if an attacker later changes
   membership or disappears. An attacker without a group tag produces
   no relation cause and the report completes silently.

3. **Deliver reports through the existing mechanics.** A pending incident
   report uses the reporter's explicit group scope. With a radio, use the
   existing broadcast delay and send to every other eligible living
   radio-bearing member of that group. Without a radio, reuse the
   rank-split walk/transfer phases to reach same-group members in person.
   Incident recipients are selected by group membership, not definition.
   Keep water notification's current recipient rules and behavior intact;
   share mechanics without mixing its knowledge or completion state with
   incidents.

   Witnessing and in-person candidate selection are qualified against the
   acting unit's page. Revalidate sender/recipient existence, life,
   membership, and applicable radio or same-page proximity conditions at
   delivery. A stale target, page change, or equal coordinates on another
   page cannot complete a walking transfer.

4. **Escalate only after successful communication.** Success means actual
   delivery to at least one other eligible living recipient. An elapsed
   broadcast delay with zero recipients installs nothing and does not
   mark the incident reported; preserve it for a later eligible attempt.
   The sender dying during walking, transfer, or broadcasting cannot
   complete a report. A vanished delivery target can be reselected
   without losing the incident. A previously completed report remains
   effective if its sender subsequently dies.

   Successful delivery installs
   `<reporter group tag> -> <captured attacker group tag>: hostile` under
   an incident-derived cause identity. Victim and witness reports of the
   same incident and pair are idempotent; distinct incidents remain
   distinct. Mark the incident reported on informed units without creating
   records on unreached units. Repeated polling, delivery, or loading a
   completed incident must not add another cause or restart its completed
   report.

5. **Apply the approved group-wide relation.** Before successful delivery,
   local retaliation installs no reverse team cause and does not trigger
   the escalation lock. Existing unrelated live/base relations still
   apply. After delivery, every member sharing the report's source group
   tag regards the attacker group as hostile through the existing relation
   authority, including members on other pages with no incident record.
   They may acquire eligible attackers under FTS-5's normal perception,
   page, and combat rules; the report grants no remote vision or target
   location. FTS-6E's reverse-hostility lock then prevents ordinary
   Hold/Cancel from clearing either direction. No per-recipient exception
   is added to relation precedence or acquisition.

6. **Persist knowledge and pending work.** Extend the existing `unit_ai`
   component, validator, schema version, and tracked compatibility fixture.
   Preserve all versions supported when this PR starts; older payloads
   without incident fields load with empty incident state, without
   manufacturing historical reports. Account for schema changes landed by
   the prerequisites or #2561 rather than assuming today's version number.

   Preserve incident identity, captured tags, observation deduplication,
   reported state, and pending phase/timing. A load mid-walk, mid-transfer,
   or mid-broadcast resumes the same next decision when recipients remain
   eligible; post-report load does not report again. Classify every new
   field in `docs/persistence_state_inventory.md`, including any derived
   or transient state. New resolvable entity references participate in
   the shared typed-reference schema and reconciliation contract. Keep
   immutable historical identity separate from live pointers: reconciling
   a missing attacker or target must not rewrite the cause identity,
   replace captured tags, or erase a valid pending incident. Preserve
   whole-session replacement and exit-to-menu cleanup.

7. **Preserve existing combat behavior.** Keep Haskell last-attacker
   fields runtime-only, with the ten-second retaliation window, in-combat
   swap, and lash-out unchanged. Do not change what an Attack order
   installs or how Hold creates a position hold. Do not consume the UI's
   drained combat/injury event streams as incident storage.

8. **Complete documentation in this PR.** Align the faction design's
   “Local awareness and team knowledge”, D-13/D-18/D-37, and FTS-6A
   descriptions with the approved distinction between group relation and
   delivered incident details. Document the implemented contract, state
   classification, and validation evidence before final PR review and
   merge. Do not change the design's processing-status markers or defer
   required documentation to a separate landing.
