## 1. Unit-AI post-load reference reconciliation

### [#1589] PRR-1. Post-load unit-AI reconciliation scrubs only a subset of its persisted references

> **Captured note:** Make `unitAi.onSaveLoaded` reconcile the same complete typed-reference schema that `unit_ai_save_refs.lua` persists and reports. A surviving AI row must not leave stale craft, repair, pickup, forage, or harvest references merely because the original #195 scrub predates those fields.

**Verification:** Verified structurally; the player-visible consequence varies by reference family and was not reproduced through a real save/load. PR #229 established a post-load orphan prune and nested-reference scrub for the unit-AI fields that existed at the time. The persisted schema has since expanded to nested craft-bill, station, item-instance, ground-item, loot-list, and location references. Its comments and the later #900 contract still promise that dangling references on surviving rows are cleared during reconciliation, but `scrubStaleRefs` only walks the two original flat field lists plus treatment and delivery tables. Location memories received their own specialized scrub; craft jobs, repair jobs, pickup orders, forage targets/loot, and harvest loot did not. The focused persistence suite validates their typed wire forms but never drives this reconciliation boundary for them.

**Evidence:**

- Issue #195 / PR #229 made `onSaveLoaded` the boundary that removes stale per-id Lua state after engine-side entity filtering. Its surviving contract was restated by closed issue #900: absent-owner rows are dropped during per-entity apply, while stale nested references on surviving rows are still cleared afterward.
- `scripts/unit_ai.lua:383-410` implements `scrubStaleRefs`. It checks `AI_UNIT_REF_FIELDS`, `AI_BUILDING_REF_FIELDS`, `treatClaim.patient`, `treatPending.uid`, `deliveryClaim.bid`, and `deliveryPendingTarget.bid`, then returns. It has no branch for any craft, repair, pickup, forage, or harvest reference.
- `scripts/unit_ai.lua:425-453` nevertheless describes the remaining reconciliation responsibility as the nested-reference scrub on every surviving row and calls only that partial helper. `knownLocations` is the sole newer reference family with a separate specialized scrub at `:454-459`.
- `scripts/unit_ai_save_refs.lua:21-24` shows that the two flat lists cover only four unit fields (`attackTargetUid`, `retreatThreatUid`, `notifyTarget`, `lungeTarget`) and two building fields (`buildTarget`, `storeTarget`). Nested references are intentionally enumerated outside those lists.
- `scripts/unit_ai_save_refs.lua:110-160` reports the complete persisted graph: the flat fields and treatment/delivery references, plus `craftJob.billId`, `craftJob.bid`, `repairJob.instanceId`, optional `repairJob.bid`, `pickupOrder.gid`, ground `forageTarget.gid`, every `forageLoot`/`harvestLoot` entry, and page-qualified `knownLocations`.
- `scripts/unit_ai_save_refs.lua:51-59` says dangling references are tolerated by validation because they are cleared by `scrubStaleRefs/onSaveLoaded`, and instructs new nested fields to mirror the reconciliation walk. That statement is false for the already-listed craft, repair, pickup, forage, and harvest fields.
- `docs/persistence_state_inventory.md:441` describes `unit_ai`'s post-apply responsibility as both the orphan prune and **the** nested-reference scrub, without documenting that most reference kinds wait for action-specific self-healing instead.
- `tools/lua_orphan_prune_probe.py:263-293` tests the post-load scrub with only `attackTargetUid` and `treatPending`. It prints a general “stale nested references scrubbed” success even though it never plants one of the schema's job, item-instance, bill, or ground-item edges.
- `test-headless/Test/Headless/Lua/SaveModules.hs` thoroughly covers wrapping, validation, migration, reference enumeration, and per-entity application for the newer fields. The focused run passed 82 examples, but repository search finds no case invoking `unitAi.onSaveLoaded` with a stale `craftJob`, `repairJob`, `pickupOrder`, `forageTarget`, `forageLoot`, or `harvestLoot` reference.
- The adjacent pending `LUA-3` in `docs/lua_script_findings.md` concerns module-local claim/priority tables outside `aiState`; it does not own these persisted fields inside a surviving AI row. Tracker and pending-report searches for `scrubStaleRefs`, the omitted field names, and incomplete `onSaveLoaded` nested-reference reconciliation found no other owner.

**Handoff context:**

- **Current behavior:** After a replacement load, `unitAi.onSaveLoaded` drops AI rows whose owning unit did not survive and clears its original direct/treatment/delivery references. A surviving row retains dangling craft-bill/station, repair-item/station, pickup, forage, and harvest identifiers until an action-specific path happens to notice them; those paths differ in timing and behavior, and the reconciliation log counts none of them as scrubbed.
- **Expected behavior:** Every tolerated dangling reference declared by the component's typed schema is resolved or cleared at the one post-publication reconciliation boundary, using the correct session-global or owning-page identity scope. Nested job/claim state is dropped coherently rather than clearing one required subfield and leaving a malformed live job table.
- **Scope and constraints:** Surfaced from PR #229 / issue #195 and its current #900/#764 descendants. Preserve the non-blocking tolerated-dangling policy, exact per-entity apply, typed wire schema and migrations, page-qualified craft-bill/ground-item/location identities, in-place `aiState` table identity, and action-specific cleanup semantics. Prefer one schema-driven or deliberately shared reconciliation walk over a fourth hand-maintained enumeration; if survivor context lacks bills, item instances, or per-page ground items, extend the reconciliation context explicitly rather than resolving page-scoped ids through whichever world is active.
- **Remaining uncertainty:** The engine's monotonic allocators and several action utilities reduce immediate collision risk, and some stale jobs self-clear on their next evaluation. A focused real-load fixture should determine the observable effect of each omitted family, especially a surviving craft job whose bill remains but station does not, a repair job whose item instance vanished, and same-numbered ground-item ids on two pages. The contract/coverage mismatch itself is direct even if the processor decides to narrow the promised scrub instead of expanding it.
