## Requirements

Create only `assets/textures/flora/saguaro/sprout_dead.png` as new artwork:
**48×48 RGBA**, with both fully transparent and opaque pixels.

Match the landed saguaro family: hard pixel edges, compact shading,
restrained palette, transparent background, and no soil, dirt mound,
painted shadow, or scenery. Preserve the living reference's ground-contact
row, canvas placement, and recognizable species silhouette.

The reference is `sprout.png`: a narrow, unbranched young saguaro.
After death it is shrivelled and collapsed inward, desiccated tan-brown to
grey with ribs showing and no green tissue. It must read as a dead juvenile,
not a scaled-down adult skeleton or a healthy sprout merely recoloured.
It may slump or narrow while retaining the original footprint and base row.
