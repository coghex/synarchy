## Background

After #2517 fluid tops render as flat whole-z steps, #2520 supplies exact
signed eighth-z surfaces, and #2525 supplies eight owner-approved level
masks. This issue integrates those prerequisites in one implementation PR:
every fluid top uses its exact height and matching mask, and visible sides
terminate at the neighbouring fluid or terrain surface.

Verified against `master` `5b677136fdac` (2026-09-10), before those
prerequisites:

- `src/World/Render/TileQuads.hs` places fluid tops from integer heights.
  `src/World/Render/SideDecoQuads.hs` emits whole-z side stacks, excludes
  Ocean, and conservatively omits edges against unloaded neighbours.
- `src/World/Render/Quads.hs` uses integer slice/depth visibility, suppresses
  Lake/Ocean tops under ice, and renders ice at its independent `icSurface`.
  `src/World/Fluid/Ice.hs` derives a base from terrain and fluid, puts drape
  ice one z above it, and caps basin thickness at 20 z.
- Structural masks travel through startup preload, world-view loading,
  initial world binding, Lua texture-name parsing, and the world texture
  command. Late structural rebinding invalidates cached quads.
- The shader uses face-map alpha for silhouette and RGB for directional
  lighting (`src/Engine/Graphics/Vulkan/ShaderCode.hs`). The #2525 masks
  already include side pixels: their top diamond stays unchanged and their
  side extrusion is `2 * level` pixels.

The prerequisites must land before implementation. Their absence from the
snapshot above does not expand this issue into state migration or asset
creation.

## Requirements

1. Load and bind all eight approved masks through the complete existing
   route: `scripts/startup_loader.lua`,
   `worldView.ensureStructuralTextures`, initial binding in
   `scripts/world_manager.lua`, `worldView.rebindStructural`,
   `parseTextureType` in `src/Engine/Scripting/Lua/Material.hs`,
   `World.Texture.Types`, `World.Render.Textures.Types`, and
   `handleWorldSetTextureCommand`. Each level resolves to its documented
   mask handle on both initial binding and late rebinding. Preserve quad,
   zoom, and background cache invalidation so late loads become visible
   without camera movement. `tools/check_texture_paths.py` must see every
   asset reference.
2. River, Lake, Lava, and Ocean select masks using the exact `topFillLevel`
   helper from #2520: levels 1..8, with every exact multiple mapped to 8.
   Never select by float rounding. Place the flat top at the exact surface
   height, converting to floating point only at the final rendering
   boundary. Neighbouring heights do not slope the top.
3. For each camera-visible edge, compare exact owning and neighbouring
   fluid planes; a dry neighbour contributes its terrain surface. Full-z
   and partial side segments must cover exactly the exposed interval,
   without gaps or duplicate coverage. This contract applies to the
   combined silhouette of the selected top mask and separate side
   geometry: account for side pixels already present in the mask. Unequal
   left/right neighbour heights must terminate independently. Equal or
   higher neighbours produce no exposed side, including an enclosed
   equal-height cell. An unloaded neighbour produces no invented edge.
   Preserve the approved mask files; geometry integration owns any
   clipping or coverage coordination needed to meet this contract.
4. Ocean uses the same level-selection and side-geometry path as the other
   fluids; remove the Ocean exclusion from `waterSideFaceQuads`. Generated
   Ocean remains level 8 and has no exposed sides beside equal neighbours.
   Edited or runtime partial Ocean uses the matching mask and exact sides.
   Existing differences in material, tint, and ice-cover policy remain.
5. Retain #2520's integer-ceiling convention for fluid top slice ownership.
   For exact surface `u`, slice `S`, and effective depth `D`, the vertical
   visibility window is `S - D <= surfaceCeilingZ u <= S`; screen-space
   culling uses the actual fractional placement. Side visibility and sort
   ordering must compose correctly with those tops. Equivalent geometry
   must agree within a chunk, across an ordinary loaded seam, and across
   the cylindrical U seam at all four camera facings. Use the production
   canonical neighbour lookup, including its unloaded-neighbour behavior.
6. Preserve existing ice elevations and modes. Ice renders at `icSurface`,
   not at the fluid ceiling. Where the ice base consumes a fluid height,
   use its documented integer ceiling: the base remains the maximum of
   terrain and that ceiling; drape remains base + 1, and basin ice retains
   its existing fill-level choice and 20-z thickness cap. Dry drape/basin
   behavior and whole-z ice results stay unchanged. Retain Lake/Ocean top
   suppression under existing ice cover, including partial fluid cells.
7. Preserve terrain slope rendering, the terrain-bed rule, zoom-map water
   classification, existing fluid tint values, and tinting policy. This is
   a rendering integration, with no new worldgen or fluid dynamics policy.
8. Add GPU-free coverage under the exact headless group
   `World.Render.FluidLevels`, using production geometry, visibility,
   lookup, and binding paths rather than test-local copies of their rules.
   Cover all levels and exact multiples; negative absolute surfaces; zero
   as a wet whole-z plane over lower terrain; fractional vertex positions;
   same-z mixed neighbours; full stacks with partial caps; multi-z drops;
   unequal left/right neighbours; enclosed equal-height cells; sort
   ordering; all facings; ordinary and U-seam parity; unloaded neighbours;
   and Ocean/Lake geometry parity. Include initial/late handle binding and
   cache invalidation, plus covered partial Lake/Ocean and dry drape/basin
   ice regressions. Test actual production slice/depth decisions on both
   sides of each boundary. For example, with `S = 2`, `D = 2`, exact units
   `u = -1, 0, 1, 16` pass the vertical window, while `u = -8, -9, 17`
   fail. Inspect vertex/coverage endpoints as well as counts; quad counts
   alone do not prove the combined rendered silhouette.
9. Supply a focused, reproducible offscreen capture driver and owner-review
   scene. Show levels 1..8 beside dry terrain, adjacent mixed levels,
   unequal left/right neighbours, an enclosed equal-height cell, full-z
   stacks with partial caps, multi-z drops, and River/Lake/Lava/partial
   Ocean variants. Include loaded seams and the ice regressions. Capture
   all four facings at representative day and night lighting. Use exact
   fixture/edit state from #2520; do not assume its legacy integer Lua
   queries provide an exact-unit setter. Capture the prerequisite-complete
   base and implementation with the same scene recipe. The owner must
   approve the integrated result before final review and merge.
10. Update repo-wide comments and documentation that describe current
    fluid rendering, including obsolete whole-z placement and two-z side
    rules. Preserve accurate historical evidence, migration explanations,
    and earlier delivery-phase descriptions. Do not disposition findings
    reports or change their status markers. Required documentation,
    reproducible capture evidence, and the recorded owner verdict are
    completion criteria of this same code PR, finished before final review
    and merge. Do not defer them to a later documentation landing.

## Out of scope

- Generated-river eighth-level progression (DFL-5); generated rivers still
  use whole-z planes at level 8 in this slice.
- Exact-unit public diagnostics, dump, or cursor fields (DFL-6).
- Save-format changes, new fluid dynamics, or changes to ice generation
  policy, ice modes, or ice elevations beyond the prerequisite conversion.
- Animated interpolation, waves, foam, refraction, or smoothing.
- Eighth-level depth on zoom-map pixels.
- New masks, mask edits, material recolours, or tint-policy changes;
  #2525 owns the approved assets.
